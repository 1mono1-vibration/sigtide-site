Privacy

Last updated 4 November 2026
The short version. SigTide has no accounts, no ads and no cookies of its own. We count page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not follow you around the web. If you subscribe to our email, your address is kept by our newsletter provider and used only to send you that email.

What we measure on this site

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see which pages people read and where they came from. It counts page views, the page that linked to us, the country, the browser and device type, and how fast the page loaded. We only ever see totals, never a profile of a person.

Cloudflare states that it uses no cookies or local storage for this, and does not "fingerprint" visitors by their IP address, browser or any other data. We do not run Google Analytics, advertising pixels or retargeting tags.

We also use Google Search Console, which tells us which Google searches showed our pages. It works from Google's own search data and puts nothing in your browser.

If you subscribe to the email

The signup form is provided by Beehiiv, our newsletter provider. When you subscribe, Beehiiv stores your email address so it can send you the email you asked for. Like most newsletter tools, Beehiiv can record whether an email was opened or a link in it was clicked. Its privacy policy covers what it collects.

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. To have your address deleted completely, write to hello@sigtide.com.

What we never do

  • We never sell or share your email address.
  • We never ask for passwords, exchange logins, API keys or money.
  • We never send you anything you did not sign up for.

Contact

Questions about this page, or anything else: hello@sigtide.com. A person reads every email.
