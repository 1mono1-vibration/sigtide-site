Privacy

Last updated 10 November 2026

The short version. SigTide has no accounts, no ads and no cookies of its own. If you sign up for our email, we keep your address and the coin you picked, and use them only to send you what you asked for.

What we measure on this site

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see which pages people read and where they came from. It counts page views, the page that linked to us, the country, the browser and device type, and how fast the page loaded. We only ever see totals, never a profile of a person. Cloudflare states that it uses no cookies or local storage for this, and does not fingerprint visitors.

We also use Google Search Console, which tells us which Google searches showed our pages. It works from Google's own search data and puts nothing in your browser. We do not run Google Analytics, advertising pixels or retargeting tags.

If you sign up for the email

  • When you touch the form, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a person, not a bot, is filling it in. It loads only then.
  • The form sends your email address and the coin you picked, if any, to our sign-up service, which runs on Cloudflare. It sends you one email with a confirm button, and nothing else happens until you tap it.
  • When you confirm, your address and coin are stored by Resend, our email provider (EU region). Resend sends the Monday letter, and an email the day your coin switches. Like most email tools, it can record whether an email was delivered, opened, or a link in it was clicked.
  • Every email has a one-tap unsubscribe link. To have your address deleted completely, write to hello@sigtide.com.

What we never do

  • Your email address is never sold, rented or shared.
  • We never ask for passwords, exchange logins, API keys or money.
  • We never send you anything you did not sign up for.

Contact

Questions about this page, or anything else: hello@sigtide.com. A person reads every email.
