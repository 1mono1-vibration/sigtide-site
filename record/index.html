How we measure

How it is computed
  • A coin is IN while its 10-day average price is above its 44-day average. Below that, it is OUT, in cash.
  • We use daily closing prices. The rule is checked once a day.
  • Each day's state applies from the next day, so no day uses a later price.
  • Every switch costs 0.15%. Just holding costs nothing.
  • Just held means equal amounts in every coin, rebalanced daily. Each coin joins the average from its first price.
  • Since Jun 15, 2026 we save each coin's daily chart score before the next day: 162 days and 7,712 rows. 2 days are missing. A few rows were later tagged or corrected. Every change is kept in our version history.
What makes these numbers look better
  • We chose the 10-day and 44-day averages after testing this same history. A fresh start may look worse.
  • The 49 coins are today's large coins. Coins that collapsed and left the top are not in the test.
  • Only 28 of the 49 coins had prices in Jan 2023. The others join the test when they list.
  • Most of this is a past test. The practice test is only 48 days old.
What the rule does not do
  • It is late on purpose. It switches after a move starts and after it turns. It never catches the top or the bottom.
  • Prices that go up and down cost money. A quick IN and OUT pays 0.15% twice, plus a small loss.
  • Most IN stretches lost money. Of 768 finished ones across the 49 coins, 279 (36%) ended higher after costs.
  • On 11 of the 28 coins with prices since Jan 2023, just holding made more. Newer coins have shorter tests.
