How we measure

How it is computed
  • A coin is IN while its 10-day average price is above its 44-day average. Below that, it is OUT, in cash.
  • We use daily closing prices. The rule is checked once a day.
  • Each day's state applies from the next day. No future data is used.
  • Every switch costs 0.15%. Just holding costs nothing.
  • Since Jun 15, 2026 we record each coin's state before the next day. That is 147 days and 6,983 rows, never edited.
What makes these numbers look better
  • We chose the 10-day and 44-day averages after testing this same history. A fresh start may look worse.
  • The 49 coins are today's large coins. Coins that collapsed and left the top are not in the test.
  • Most of this is a past test. The practice test is only 33 days old.
What the rule does not do
  • It is late on purpose. It switches after a move starts and after it turns. It never catches the top or the bottom.
  • Prices that go up and down cost money. A quick IN and OUT pays 0.15% twice, plus a small loss.
  • Most IN stretches lost money. Of 736 finished ones across the 49 coins, 267 (36%) ended higher after costs.
  • On 12 of 49 coins, just holding made more.
