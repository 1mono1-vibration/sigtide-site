What the number actually means

A score is a checklist result, not an opinion. Every coin runs through the same ten yes/no checks — is it above its 20- and 50-day averages, is the 50-day rising, is it making higher highs and higher lows, is momentum positive on MACD, is RSI in a healthy band, is it outperforming Bitcoin, does the weekly chart agree, is volume backing the up-days, and is it near its breakout level.

Each check is worth a fixed number of points, set before today's data existed. Add them up, out of 20. Nobody votes, nothing is retuned afterwards, and the same test runs on all 49 coins whether it flatters the market or embarrasses it.

Most coins fail most of the time, and that is the point — a score that passed everything would be telling you nothing.
