How to read it

RSI compares the last 14 days of rises with the falls, on a scale from 0 to 100. It describes the past. It does not forecast: strong coins can stay above 70 for weeks, and weak ones can stay below 30 on the way down.

We show daily RSI only, from the same daily closing prices the rule uses. An hourly number would be out of date within hours on a page that updates once a day.
